FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know about TagMyCredit, trade credit scoring, and the network.

01What is TagMyCredit?

The world's first B2B Trade Credit Intelligence Network. Suppliers tag the credit they give business buyers, buyers confirm it by OTP, and the network turns those tags into risk scores and default alerts. Like a FICO score for B2B trade credit.

02How does the TMC-ID work?

Every registered company gets a TMC-ID, a unique, anonymous, permanent identifier such as TMC-OM-8397-4521-01-K. Suppliers tag credit lines against it. It cannot be guessed and it cannot be reversed into your company's details.

03What is AAI (Anonymous Aggregated Intelligence)?

It's the privacy model. Data from many suppliers is combined before anyone sees it, so you get a score, a capacity traffic light, an exposure range and alerts, never another supplier's name or amounts.

04Who can see my trade credit data?

You see your own credit lines in full detail. Everyone else sees only aggregated intelligence. Exact receivables or payables, bank balances, financial statements, operating costs and individual transactions stay protected.

05How is the Trade Risk Score calculated?

It's an 8-component score from 0 to 1000, updated live from network data. Components include payment discipline, utilization, bad debt history and financial strength.

06Is TagMyCredit available in my country?

TagMyCredit is built for the GCC, starting with Saudi Arabia and the UAE. Register your company and we'll confirm availability for your market.

07What does it cost?

The Starter plan is free forever, and no credit card is required to register.

08How long does setup take?

About 3 minutes to register and get your TMC-ID. Then you can tag your first credit line.
