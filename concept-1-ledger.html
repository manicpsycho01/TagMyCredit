FAQ

Common questions.

What is TagMyCredit?

A shared network where suppliers record the credit they give business buyers. Buyers confirm each line, and every member gets a risk score and alerts in return.

How does the TMC-ID work?

Every registered company gets a permanent, anonymous ID. Credit lines are tagged against it, so your score follows your company, not your name.

What is AAI (Anonymous Aggregated Intelligence)?

You only ever see combined, anonymous information about a buyer, never which suppliers are involved or their exact amounts.

Who can see my trade credit data?

Only you see your own lines in full. Everyone else sees scores, ranges and alerts.

How is the Trade Risk Score calculated?

From 8 components, including payment discipline, utilization, bad debt history and financial strength, on a 0–1000 scale.

Is TagMyCredit available in my country?

The network is launching in the GCC, starting with Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

What does it cost?
